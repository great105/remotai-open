# Remotai name and branding

**English** · [Русский](TRADEMARKS.md)

The source license does not grant rights to the official Remotai service's
trademarks, name or logo.

You may accurately say that your project is based on Remotai and preserve its
authorship information. Use your own name for a public third-party service or
distributed fork, and identify its operator. Do not present it as the official
Remotai service or claim our maintenance or support without a separate agreement.

An installation for yourself or your own team does not require renaming.
