Privacy Policy

Updated September 7, 2026

What is Remotai for?

Remotai helps you work remotely with AI agents on your computers and servers: run tasks, see terminals and open project files. This page describes data processing in the application and cloud service.

What data does the cloud store?

  • Account and login. Internal account number, details of the selected login method, for example Telegram ID and public profile fields. Remotai does not receive correspondence and Telegram contacts.
  • Devices and access. Machine ID and name, system, Remotai version, connection status, workspace members and granted permissions.
  • Payment. Mail for the check, payment number from YuKassa, amount, status and due date. Remotai does not receive the full card number, CVC and confirmation codes. A new purchase pays for 30 days without saving the card for automatic renewal.
  • Support. The text that you send to the support chat, the time and status of the request. Do not send passwords, keys, card details or bank codes.
  • Product analytics. Site visits, downloads, registration source, UTM tags, app opens, machine connections and feature usage events. After login, the event can be associated with the internal account number. The source page address is shortened to its domain.

Terminals, agents and files

With a cloud connection, the Remotai server transmits traffic between authorized devices via TLS. Terminal contents, commands, files, screen frames, clicks, and clipboards are not saved as product analytics. The transmitted content of a remote session is not saved by the relay after delivery. Local logs and terminal history on your machine can be saved separately from the cloud.

Agents run on a connected machine. The AI ​​service you choose can receive tasks and project data according to its own rules. His account and payment are separate from Remotai. When connecting directly via a local network, session traffic does not pass through the Remotai cloud relay.

Third party services

  • Telegram used for login, widget and bot operation.
  • YuKassa processes payment. To generate a check, the email, amount and description of the purchase are sent to her.
  • The AI providers you choose process agent requests on their own terms and conditions.

Advertising pixels and advertising cookies are not used. Application settings and login information may be stored locally on your browser or device.

Data storage and management

The cloud stores the above data for account operation, access, payment and support. Backups may contain previously deleted entries during the recovery period. Deleting an app from your phone or computer does not in itself delete your cloud account.

You can unlink the device in the application, change your receipt email in your personal account and contact support for access to data or deletion of your account. Payment records may be retained for mandatory retention periods.
