1. What is a service

Remotai is a program for computer (Windows, macOS, Linux) and an application for phone and browser, which provide remote access to command line AI agents (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Kimi Code and others), terminals, files and the screen of the User’s computer. The AI agents themselves and subscriptions to them Not provided by the contractor: The user installs them and pays for them from their developers himself.

2. Free and paid parts

  • Locally - free and without time limits. Working with terminals, AI agents, files and your computer screen, including managing it on one local network, available without payment. Working with servers is a paid part.
  • Servers and Cloud - by subscription. The paid part includes built-in SSH/SFTP, port forwarding and other server connections, as well as access through the Contractor’s cloud server (hereinafter referred to as “Cloud”): connection to your computers via the Internet, notifications and PC → PC access. Server functions are also paid when connecting from a local network. VPS rental and AI provider accounts are not included in the Remotai subscription.
  • Trial period. One total 30-day trial begins on first use server functions or Cloud and gives access to the “Pro” tariff. Registration and viewing status the sample is not run. A bank card is not required for the trial period.
